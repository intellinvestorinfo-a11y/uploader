Intelligent Investor Uploader

A personal tool that uploads the owner's own educational finance videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner clicks Connect TikTok and approves access to his own account (profile name, and permission to upload and post his videos).
  2. TikTok returns him to this site, which shows a login code.
  3. The owner pastes that code into his uploader tool, which then shows the connected account's name and sends his finished video and caption to TikTok.

Connect TikTok

Permissions requested: user.info.basic (shows the connected account's name), video.upload and video.publish (sends the owner's own video and caption to his own account).
